      Papers with DOI and PubMed

      A green DOI or PMID tag means the identifier was checked against a database while preparing this page. Where no identifier is shown, the links search PubMed and Crossref for the exact title, because many of these 1897–1953 papers have no DOI.

        Enzyme names, years and attributions follow the reviews of Kresge, Simoni and Hill (2005) and Barnett (2003) and the Nobel lectures. Where sources differ the cautious wording is used, and approximate years are shown as ranges.
